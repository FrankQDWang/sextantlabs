# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Fiction authors working inside an active manuscript. They are usually revising or continuing a scene, checking continuity, deciding whether to accept a candidate line, and reviewing memory changes without leaving the writing surface.

## Product Purpose

Sextant is a writing workbench and memory system that turns accepted source material into evidence-backed story memory. Success means the author can ask for help, inspect a candidate, accept all or part of it, and see memory/review consequences without losing authorship or polluting canon.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exacting, author-sovereign. The interface should feel like a restrained writing desk with production-grade memory controls: calm enough for prose, precise enough for evidence and review work.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a marketing site, generic dashboard, chat-first assistant, colorful AI playground, or local demo. Avoid broad hero sections, decorative gradients, card-heavy explanation layouts, fake success states, and any UI that implies model output can become canon without author acceptance and evidence.

## Design Principles

1. The manuscript remains the center of gravity.
2. Every candidate action must preserve author choice and target provenance.
3. Memory and canon controls must show evidence, risk, and review status as distinct things.
4. Production behavior should replace prototype state without disrupting the quiet workbench visual language.
5. Chinese interface copy should stay concise, literary, and operational rather than promotional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus states, keyboard operation, and screen-reader labels. Preserve reduced-motion support and avoid color-only status communication for canon, pending, risk, and review states.
